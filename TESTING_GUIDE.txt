====================================================================
 FMS - TESTING GUIDE (NEW MODULES)
====================================================================

Quick reference for testing the 8 modules added on top of the
original prototype: General Ledger, Cash and Bank, Payroll, Tax,
Approval Engine, Inventory, Purchase Order, Document Management.


LOGIN ACCOUNTS NEEDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Accountant            - Almost everything: creating records, GL
                         entries, Cash & Bank, Payroll, Tax,
                         Inventory, Purchase Orders, Documents,
                         and Step 2 of Purchase Order approval.

College Administrator - Step 1 of Purchase Order approval only.

Some tests need BOTH accounts logged in at the same time. Easiest
way: open one in a normal window and the other in an Incognito
window.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GENERAL LEDGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Go to General Ledger > New Journal Entry.
  2. Add 2 lines: pick an account, put an amount in Debit; pick
     another account, same amount in Credit.
  3. Confirm it shows "Balanced" before saving, then Save.
  4. Entry appears in the table. Click View, then try Trial
     Balance - totals should say "Balanced."
  5. Try "Reverse Entry" on the entry you just made - a second,
     offsetting entry should appear.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CASH AND BANK
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. "+ New Account" - create a Bank/Cash account, link it to a GL
     account (e.g. "1000 - Cash"), give it an opening balance.
  2. "+ New Transaction" > Deposit - pick the account, a Contra
     Account (e.g. Revenue), an amount. Save.
  3. Balance should go UP, and a matching entry should appear in
     General Ledger.
  4. Try Withdrawal the same way - balance should go DOWN.
  5. Try withdrawing more than the balance - should be REJECTED
     with an error.
  6. Try Transfer between two accounts - one balance goes down,
     the other goes up.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PAYROLL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Employees tab > "+ New Employee" - add at least one.
  2. Payroll Runs tab > "+ New Payroll Run" - give it a label and
     date range.
  3. Open it > Generate Payslips - should compute SSS, PhilHealth,
     Pag-IBIG, and Tax per employee.
  4. Post to General Ledger - status becomes "Posted."
  5. Check General Ledger - one consolidated entry should appear,
     balanced.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TAX
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Go to Tax - confirm the 3 tax types (Withholding, VAT,
     Percentage Tax) are listed.
  2. "+ New Remittance" - pick a tax type, amount, and which bank
     account to pay from.
  3. Balance of that bank account goes down; entry appears in both
     Cash and Bank AND General Ledger.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. APPROVAL ENGINE   (needs both accounts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. As Accountant: Approval Engine > "+ New Test Request."
  2. As College Administrator: open the request > Approve (Step 1).
  3. Step 1 turns green; Step 2 becomes active.
  4. As Accountant: open the request > Approve (Step 2).
  5. Whole request status becomes "Approved."
  6. (Optional) Try Reject at Step 1 instead - whole request should
     finalize as "Rejected" immediately.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. INVENTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. "+ New Item" - give it a code, name, reorder level.
  2. "+ New Movement" > Stock In, some quantity. Qty on hand goes
     up.
  3. Stock Out a large quantity, close to what's on hand - item
     should show a red "Low Stock" warning once it drops to/below
     reorder level.
  4. Try a Stock Out larger than what's available - should be
     REJECTED.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PURCHASE ORDER   (needs both accounts + 1 Inventory item)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. As Accountant: "+ New Purchase Order" - supplier name, add a
     line item (pick an inventory item, quantity). Save as Draft.
  2. Open it > Submit for Approval.
  3. As College Administrator: go to Approval Engine, find the
     request, Approve.
  4. As Accountant: go to Approval Engine, Approve the same request
     (Step 2).
  5. Back in Purchase Order, open the PO - status should now be
     "Approved," with a Receive Items button.
  6. Click Receive Items.
  7. Check Inventory - quantity should have increased by the
     ordered amount.
  8. Check General Ledger - a new balanced entry should appear
     (Debit Inventory, Credit Accounts Payable).


--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. "+ Upload Document" - pick any PDF/image file, choose a
     related module. Upload.
  2. File appears in the table with correct name and size. Click
     the filename - it should open in a new tab.
  3. Try the "Filter by Module" dropdown - list should narrow down
     correctly.
  4. Delete the document - it should disappear from the list.


====================================================================
 AUTOMATED TESTS (optional, for developers)
====================================================================

Every module above also has PHPUnit tests. To run all of them at
once, open a terminal in the project folder and run:

    php artisan test

All tests should show PASS. If anything shows FAIL, note which
test and check the corresponding module before assuming the manual
testing above will work correctly.
